# Lead Auditor Verdict — Results Audit, Round 3, Round 1

I opened the raw sources myself for every BLOCKING/disputed item raised by A, B and C. Summary of my own recomputations (all done independently against `cfd/unit_cell_campaign/dataset_ledger_unitcell.csv`, `cfd/unit_cell_campaign/campaign_design.json`, `audit/provenance.csv`, `audit/decisions.md`, `manuscript/sections/numerical_method.tex` and `manuscript/tables/tab_campaign_counts.tex` / `figures/src/make_campaign_tables.py`):

**Confirmed standing (BLOCKING):**

1. **`tab_campaign_counts.tex` "at cap" column is mislabeled.** The caption and `sections/parametric_results.tex:81` both describe the 76-count column as cases "stopped at the 4000-iteration cap." The code (`figures/src/make_campaign_tables.py:27`) actually counts `iterations>=4000`. I recomputed this set from the ledger: of the cases with `iterations>=4000`, 21 have `converged=True` and ran on to 5013–18,504 iterations (e.g. C023: 18,504) — they were never "stopped at the cap," they converged normally well past it. The column conflates two different populations (genuinely envelope-stopped-near-4000 cases and normally-converged cases that merely exceed 4000 iterations in total).

2. **Undisclosed incompleteness of the calibration partition itself.** `campaign_design.json` designs 99 calibration cases (C001–C099). The ledger I read (`cfd/unit_cell_campaign/dataset_ledger_unitcell.csv`, mtime 2026-09-05 00:54) currently has 93 finished rows — C094–C099 (all OR=1.0, Re≥20) have no `DONE` file and never ran; C093 finished at 00:53:18, four minutes before the ledger's own last write, and 21 minutes after `manuscript/tables/tab_campaign_counts.tex` was last generated (00:33:12) — i.e. the campaign is still live and the published "run=92" cell is already stale. `sections/parametric_results.tex:85` states as an empirical finding: "every case at OR ≥ 0.6 exceeded the 70°C wall bound at every Reynolds level." I checked all 45 designed OR≥0.6 cases against the ledger: only 39 have actually run; the claim is asserted for 6 Re levels (OR=1.0, Re=20/40/70/100/150/250) that were never simulated. The abstract's closing sentence discloses that "withheld-coolant, withheld-load, grid and fixed-fin cases were still running... and are marked as such" — but says nothing about the calibration set itself also being incomplete, unlike every other partition, which is explicitly flagged `MISSING` throughout. This is a disclosure/consistency failure (Part B item 7) and an unsupported empirical claim (Part A item 1) at once. (Note: because the campaign is actively running, the exact current gap will keep shrinking — the defect is the non-disclosure, not a fixed headline number.)

3. **`numerical_method.tex` "Calibration campaign as performed" paragraph contradicts the rest of the manuscript.** Line 194–210 (the exact paragraph named in the audit scope) states the iteration ceiling as "12,000 iterations," with no other exception. `audit/decisions.md` (Addendum, 22:50, 4 Sept 2026) documents a third continuation pass to 20,000 iterations for five named cases (C012, C022, C023, C034, C042). `sections/parametric_results.tex:91` itself correctly states the accepted-case range as "1201 to 18,504 iterations" (case C023) — which is impossible under a hard 12,000-iteration cap. I grepped every section file: the 20,000-iteration extension is disclosed nowhere. The "as performed" paragraph does not describe what was actually performed.

4. **`audit/provenance.csv` rule-8 violation.** I checked tokens 665, 993, 1125, 1127, 1128, 1129 directly, and confirmed by parsing `parametric_campaign/doe_definition.json` that it contains no key literally named `"Q at duct Re 250 [LPM]"` or `"OR level"` — these are invented labels attached to the wrong file. The values these tokens are supposed to source are real campaign results (mass-split closure 4×10⁻⁷ %, Nu_edge=2.6 at C001, Phi MAPE 2.6%, Nu MAPE 3.6%, effective-fraction MAE 2.0pp) that only *coincidentally* equal an old empty-duct DOE design constant (`flow_rate_LPM=2.61` at Re=250, OR=0). The manuscript's printed numbers themselves are correct (both A and B independently verified this), but the provenance file — whose entire purpose is round-3's traceability requirement — cites a source that does not hold the value in the way claimed, across 6 tokens spanning 3 manuscript locations. This is a rule violation as defined by the verdict's own BLOCKING category, not a manuscript-accuracy error.

**Confirmed standing (NON-BLOCKING):**

1. `sections/verification_validation.tex:101` — "the diverged and envelope-stopped cases, whose energy balances reach 10%" — I recomputed `energy_balance_pct` for the 59 rejected calibration rows: range 0.26%–147.6%, median 5.4%; even excluding the 3 diverged cases, the range is 0.26%–53.6%. "Reach 10%" materially understates the actual spread, but it is a qualitative aside, not a specific reported statistic.
2. `sections/discussion.tex:19-21` — the "higher fin efficiency of a shorter fin" mechanism for the near-constant convective-resistance term is stated as fact with no η_fin/η_o number given in text (though I confirm it is physically correct: η_o rises ≈0.26→0.39 across accepted cases).
3. Abstract's compressed MISSING list (`main.tex:27`) omits the "cross-combinations" partition that `conclusions.tex`, `correlation_and_holdout.tex` Sec 7.1/7.3 and `tab_statistics.tex` all list explicitly — confirmed by grep; a completeness gap in a summary sentence, not a contradiction.
4. `audit/decisions.md`'s account of which OR=1 cases ran on which machine is stale relative to the now-93-row ledger (subsumed by BLOCKING item 2 above).
